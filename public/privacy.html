Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Simple summary: We do not sell your data. We do not share your data. Your business information is used only to show you relevant laws.

1. Who We Are

Paaband is a Pakistan business compliance app that helps business owners understand which laws apply to their business. We are based in Pakistan.

Contact us: paaband.app@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Paaband, we collect:

  • Email address — used for login and account management only
  • Business information — business name, type, province, city, number of employees, revenue range. This is used only to show you relevant laws.
  • App usage — which laws you viewed, compliance status. Used to improve the app.

We do not collect:

  • Bank account details or financial information
  • CNIC or national identity number
  • Location or GPS data
  • Phone contacts or call logs
  • Photos or camera data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To show you laws relevant to your business type, province, and size
  • To save your compliance progress and settings
  • To send you important compliance deadline reminders (if you opt in)
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

We use these trusted services to run the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (supabase.com)
  • Vercel — web hosting (vercel.com)

Both services are GDPR compliant and do not use your data for their own purposes.

5. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • We use Supabase Row Level Security so users can only access their own data

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correct — update your business information at any time in the app
  • Export — request an export of your data

To exercise any of these rights email us at paaband.app@gmail.com

7. Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

8. Children

Paaband is intended for business owners aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by email or in-app notification.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy contact us at:

  • Email: paaband.app@gmail.com
  • Website: verri-5.vercel.app
